# CHB-MIT seizure EEG cohort

Correspondence: revised manuscript Fig. 10(b), Figs. S17–S18 and Table S16 (27 November 2026). This compact archive contains final pooled and sensitivity tables, not the CHB-MIT EDF collection or all final event-level NNDR ratios.

## Data and window calculation

Obtain the [CHB-MIT Scalp EEG Database](https://physionet.org/content/chbmit/1.0.0/) under its posted ODC Attribution 1.0 terms; cite Shoeb (2009) and PhysioNet. Official `chbXX-summary.txt` files give independent onset and offset annotations. `cohort_inclusion_exclusion.csv` and `Seizure_EEG_cohort_reconciliation.csv` identify the final event selection. The final analysis has 182 seizures in 135 recordings from 23 independent patients; 24 folder identifiers reduce to 23 because chb21 and chb01 refer to one patient.

The archived estimator sources `scripts/nndr_seizure_stage6_cohort.py` (10 s) and `nndr_seizure_stage6_40s_sensitivity_empty_event_fixed.py` (40 s) use the fixed 16-bipolar-channel `CORE_16` montage declared in source. They read official annotations, filter 0.5–15 Hz, apply a 60 Hz notch, downsample 256 to 32 Hz, and center channels in the fitting sequence. Trailing window endpoints advance by 2 s; the main pooled analysis uses 40 s. Relative ridge is 0.003 times mean channel variance and trace-preserving shrinkage α=0.05. M2 and M3 are extracted independently. Full/reduced stability and principal-real-log criteria govern admissibility; undefined values remain missing. The source also contains separate model-order spotchecks; these are not evidence that synthetic adequacy screening was applied to the empirical cohort. The 10 s script is an archived sensitivity implementation, **not** the final 40 s pooled aggregation script.

For a full calculation, put the copied script in a CHB-MIT directory containing subject folders and official summary files, or set its `DATA_ROOT_OVERRIDE` to that directory, then run `python nndr_seizure_stage6_cohort.py` or `python nndr_seizure_stage6_40s_sensitivity_empty_event_fixed.py`. Check top-level `WINDOW_SECONDS`, `MAX_FILES_TO_PROCESS`, `RUN_M3` and output settings before running. These commands execute archived stages; neither alone regenerates the final 182-event pooled table. The final pooled event-level normalization/aggregation source and its event-level ratio CSV were not found in this compact handoff, so a complete raw-to-Fig.-10(b) command cannot honestly be supplied. Do not treat the earlier 184-event historical table as final.

## Alignment and aggregation

Every diagnostic is assigned to its trailing-window **end**, τ_end=t_end−t_onset. The original sensitivity code labels baseline endpoints from -180 through -40 s and fully ictal windows entirely inside the annotation; another seizure or its guard excludes contaminated baselines. For the final pooled 40 s series, each seizure/method is divided by the median of its own positive, admissible pre-seizure baseline, then seizure values are combined within an independent patient and patient medians/IQRs are pooled at each endpoint. The displayed range is -50 to +100 s. A normalized value of one denotes that seizure's baseline; it is distinct from the mathematical R_A=1 amplification threshold. Missing or inadmissible estimates are neither zero-filled nor interpolated. `pooled_manuscript_diagnostics.csv` gives endpoint, method, diagnostic, value kind, median, Q25, Q75, patient and seizure counts. The exact final baseline gate used by the absent pooled aggregation source remains unresolved beyond the archived stage's [-180,-40] s rule.

EEG activity is the smoothed absolute amplitude of the 16-channel mean field, pooled event→patient→cohort; `scripts/aggregate_eeg_activity.py` implements this from `individual_eeg_activity.csv` and merges chb21 with chb01. It writes files, so run it on a disposable copy of this directory. Response-direction support is exp(-Σpᵢln pᵢ)/N, pᵢ=r̂ᵢ²/||r̂||². One-step splitting is |λ₁−λ₂|/|λ₁+λ₂| for the reduced one-step map, distinct from rate splitting.

## Window-duration sensitivity

Fig. S18 matches events eligible at **both** 10 and 40 s: a positive admissible baseline and fully ictal windows at each length. The archived stage uses baseline endpoints [-180,-40] s, 2 s steps, and the criteria recorded in `EEG_window_sensitivity_matched_events.csv`. For each event/recording and method, D_W=log₂(median fully-ictal ratio / median baseline ratio). Recording results are aggregated within patient; `EEG_window_sensitivity_patients.csv` records patient contrasts at both lengths and their paired 40-minus-10 difference. `EEG_window_sensitivity_summary.csv` and `EEG_window_sensitivity_table_for_SM.csv` preserve medians, quartiles and counts. This is a matched **cohort** comparison; Fig. S15's 10/20 s curves concern one representative recording and are separate.

## Archived-table route and verification

Run `python scripts/verify_archived.py` here; it reads final tables without changing them. It checks 182 events, 135 recordings, 23 independent identities, median duration 46.5 s, 22,878 pooled rows and Table S16 availability: normalized R_A M2 20,468/M3 22,587, R_σ M2 20,534/M3 22,621. Matched-event counts are R_A 79/91 and R_σ 83/93 (M2/M3), spanning 21 patients. Across endpoints within median seizure duration, final median normalized R_A rounds to 1.02 M2 and 1.04 M3; largest median-curve values are 1.12 and 1.10. `Seizure_EEG_counts_FINAL.csv` supplies screening and row counts; `EEG_window_sensitivity_*` files supply matched events and patients. The final event-level ratio and baseline tables are unavailable here, so final pooled medians cannot be recomputed independently from intermediate ratios. The cohort does not show a consistent R_A increase at onset and does not establish an early-warning signal. Windows overlap; patient-level bootstrap intervals, where used, hold selected planes fixed and are approximate.
