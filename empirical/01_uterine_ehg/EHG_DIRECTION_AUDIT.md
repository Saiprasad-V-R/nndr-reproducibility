# M3 response-direction support audit

The packaged `ehg_final_v2_full_cohort.py` is byte-identical to the original source in the EHG workspace. Its `m3_plane` chooses the extreme eigenvectors of `FFᵀ−FᵀF`, then runs seeded projected M2 optimization **within the resulting 2D plane**. The response direction is the first resulting column; support is `||r||₁/(sqrt(16)||r||₂)`. The packaged and archived metadata use the same seed (20261118), 10 M2 restarts, 160 iterations, ridge 0.003 and shrinkage 0.10. Their recorded NumPy/SciPy/Python versions differ (2.0.2/1.13.1/3.9.6 here versus 2.4.6/1.18.1/3.12.14 for the archive).

Of 679 M3 windows, 100 have support differences above 1e-5; the maximum is 0.0746174. For these 100, the median absolute difference in the two reduced matrices' diagonal entries is large in the original basis, but swapping their diagonal positions brings the median summed diagonal difference to 0.000643. Across all windows, maximum differences in reduced-matrix trace, determinant and squared Frobenius norm are below 2.2e-9. The maximum R_A difference is 4.75e-6 and the maximum R_sigma difference is 7.12e-5. M2 support differs by at most 1.49e-8.

The mismatched windows do **not** show a generally collapsed commutator eigengap: median extreme-to-next eigengap divided by the largest absolute commutator eigenvalue is 0.945, versus 0.941 in matching windows. This supports the same selected plane. The discrepancy is in the directional basis chosen by the coordinate-dependent, seeded within-plane optimization; its starting coordinates can depend on eigensolver basis/sign conventions. Different numerical library versions are a plausible trigger, but the archived numerical environment was not reconstructed here. The slightly different attained objectives in affected windows reinforce that the direction optimization, not the amplification formula, differs.

The original manuscript support series remains in `reference_data/windows.csv` and the manuscript plotting table. The new raw-recording run's support stays in `results/records/ice039_p_3of6/windows.csv`. Neither series was substituted for the other. The original M3 ordering, optimizer tolerances and support formula remain unchanged. R_A and R_sigma calculations were not modified.
