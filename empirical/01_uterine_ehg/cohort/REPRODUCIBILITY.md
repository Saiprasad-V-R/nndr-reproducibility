# Uterine EHG cohort

Correspondence: revised manuscript Fig. 10(a), Figs. S13–S14 and Table S17 (27 November 2026). This directory contains archived cohort summaries and copies of the original calculation code. It does not contain the PhysioNet waveforms or all 78,776 window rows.

## Data and calculation

Acquire the [Icelandic 16-electrode EHG database](https://physionet.org/content/ehgdb/1.0.0/) under its posted ODC Attribution 1.0 terms; cite Alexandersson et al., *Scientific Data* 2, 150017 (2015), doi:10.1038/sdata.2015.17. `data/dataset_manifest.csv` maps the 123 recording stems; `dataset_coverage.csv` maps stems to 45 subject IDs. The original calculation is `scripts/ehg_final_v2_full_cohort.py`; its `RUN_MODE="dataset"` consumes WFDB `.hea/.dat/.atr` files placed beside the script and writes `ehg_final_outputs/`. Copy the script to the dataset directory before `python ehg_final_v2_full_cohort.py`, or set its top-level `DATA_DIR` and `OUTDIR` to local paths. This is an expensive full-cohort route and was not rerun here. Dependencies: Python 3, numpy, scipy, pandas, matplotlib, wfdb; see `../../requirements.txt`.

The source crops 200 s from the start and 60 s from the end, selects all available 16 EHG channels, converts mV to µV, applies a 0.08–2.0 Hz fourth-order filter, resamples to 10 Hz, and forms a Hilbert-envelope activity signal. NNDR uses trailing 60 s windows every 5 s, relative ridge `3e-3` times mean channel variance, trace-preserving shrinkage α=0.10, M2 directed-coupling optimization and M3 commutator extraction. The source checks full and reduced stability, principal-log admissibility, rank/conditioning, and leaves inadmissible estimates missing. Its crop and ridge comments call these settings provisional; they are the actual saved calculation settings, not an inferred manuscript value. No empirical adequacy test is claimed.

Activity is smoothed over 120 s. Within each recording, windows above its 80th activity percentile are high; windows at or below its median are baseline. The source takes medians of admissible ratios in each class, requires at least eight finite windows in each class, and calculates high-minus-baseline and log₂(high/baseline) when both medians are positive. Recording contrasts are combined by the median within each subject, then across subjects. This makes subject the independent unit. `scripts/recover_fig11_original_metrics.py` recovers the corrected final 44-subject common-gate Fig. S13 statistics and reaction geometry from the original cached windows; it requires those caches and is not run from the compact archive alone. The older `data/dataset_record_effects.csv` and `dataset_subject_effects.csv` use a method-specific gate and **must not** be substituted for the corrected common-gate Table S17 values.

Table S17 uses subject-resampled percentile bootstrap intervals for the median, with planes fixed. Per-window Spearman correlation links activity to ratio; Cliff's delta compares high and baseline classes. The archived `subject_statistical_panels.csv` holds both, while `reaction_geometry_subject.csv` holds response-coordinate mean-field reconstruction R² and absolute alignment with the uniform mode. Response support uses the EHG normalization stated in manuscript Eq. S11. One-step multiplier splitting is |λ₁−λ₂|/|λ₁+λ₂| for the reduced operator, distinct from rate splitting and K. M2–M3 agreement is assessed over paired subjects, not overlapping windows.

Fig. S14 uses independent C markers: 137 markers from 59 recordings and 30 subjects. In `scripts/ehg_C_timing_placebo_180s_v1.py`, marker-centered windows are [-20,20] s and the pre-marker reference is [-120,-60] s, both relative to marker time, requiring three valid endpoints per phase. Uniform and activity-quintile-weighted controls each have 2,000 draws. True-marker exclusion is compared at 120 and 180 s; pseudo-markers are at least 240 s apart. Possible-contraction markers have a 90 s exclusion and artifacts 150 s. Source seed and full parameters are retained in the script. Activity matching is approximate, not exact. The 120 s precursor is also included.

## Archived-table route

Run `python scripts/verify_archived.py` from this directory. It reads `subject_RA.csv`, `subject_Rsigma.csv`, `EHG_counts.csv` and `marker_primary_M3.csv` without changing them. The subject files contain per-subject baseline/high medians, contrasts, log₂ ratios, correlation, Cliff's delta, support and reconstruction quantities. `dataset_coverage.csv` and `record_activity_admissibility_exact.csv` give recording/window counts and exclusions. `marker_placebo_replicates_{120s,180s}.csv` preserve control draws. The exact full window-to-subject regeneration requires the original per-record `windows.csv` cache, which is deliberately not bundled.

## Verification and limits

The archived final tables give 123 recordings, 45 subjects, 44 common-gate subjects, 78,776 windows, and admissible counts M2=56,746 and M3=59,697. Table S17 subject contrasts round to R_A M3 0.45 (36 positive), M2 0.53 (39); R_σ M3 1.09 (39), M2 1.21 (40). Manuscript intervals are [0.24,0.58], [0.28,0.67], [0.75,1.34], [0.90,1.60], respectively. The exact bootstrap resample stream for the final common-gate intervals is not bundled as a compact file; those intervals are manuscript checkpoints, not independently reconstructed here. Marker controls are association tests, not causal evidence of contraction. Overlapping windows are dependent; bootstrap intervals hold fitted planes fixed and are approximate.
