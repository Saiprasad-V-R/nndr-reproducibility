## Lightweight numerical checks

The same real stable, complex stable, near-threshold, defective, near-repeated and negative-real 2x2 operators were passed to `shared/nndr_thresholds.py` and toolkit `thresholds.py`. R_A and R_sigma agreed to floating-point precision in every admissible case. Both leave R_A undefined for the negative-real principal-log case. A seeded small ridge fit agreed exactly after passing the same absolute lambda. These checks do not establish equivalence of every optimizer trajectory or every empirical support convention.
