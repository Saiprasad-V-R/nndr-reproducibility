\caption{\textbf{Nonlinear tests against independently known tangent dynamics.}
(a) Continuous-time variance-response diagnostic $R_A$ for directed FitzHugh--Nagumo (FHN) dynamics, calculated using the known window-averaged, time-ordered tangent map $T_w$ (black) or finite-data local VAR estimates (M2, orange; M3, green). Each point is the median over admissible subject-level fits; missing values are not assigned zero. The shaded interval denotes the imposed forcing pulse, $t\in[23,30]$, and the horizontal dotted line indicates $R_A=1$. Where fewer than six of eight subjects yield admissible $R_A$, we show isolated symbols without connecting lines. The labels $1/8$ and $4/8,5/8$ refer to the number of admissible fits for M3 at $t=30$ and for M2 and M3, respectively, at $t=38$. Thus the isolated post-pulse maxima do not establish a consistent response across subjects.
(b) Relative discrepancy $\delta_F=\lVert F_{\mathrm{ref}}-T_w\rVert_F/\lVert T_w\rVert_F$ for the independent-ensemble statistical transition $F_{\mathrm{stat}}=C_1C_0^{-1}$ and the exponential of the mean Jacobian $e^{\Delta t\bar J}$. Symbols indicate medians and segments indicate interquartile ranges across analyzed windows on a logarithmic axis. The three reference constructions are not equivalent in general nonlinear stochastic dynamics.
(c) Largest principal angle $\theta_{\max}(\widehat Q,Q_{T_w})$ between an inferred M2 or M3 plane and the M2-selected plane of the known tangent reference. Error bars are exploratory 95\% percentile intervals obtained by resampling the eight independent simulated subjects, retaining each subject's overlapping windows as a group. Large angles for weakly identified near-normal planes need not imply equally large error in the physical gain.
(d) Number of inadmissible real-rate $R_A$ estimates among 104 analyzed windows for each nonlinear family and method (M2/M3). Families are FHN, directed Stuart--Landau below ($\mathrm{SL}^{-}$) and above ($\mathrm{SL}^{+}$) Hopf, and a symmetric-gradient double-well system. All systems have $N=8$, eight independent simulated subjects per family, ten estimation trajectories per subject, observation interval $\Delta t=0.2$, window length 60 and stride 20. One gradient-control M2 fit incorrectly exceeds $R_A=1$ and is not rejected by the transferred model-adequacy screen. The numerical results come from frozen V19 T09 outputs; inadmissible rates are never clipped or imputed.}
