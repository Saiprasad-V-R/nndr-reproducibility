\caption{Synthetic VAR(1) trajectories across the corrected reduced threshold.
The real $N=200$ transition matrices are generated from the fixed-spectrum
family of Eq.~(44), with $\rho(F)=0.95$, independent isotropic innovations
of standard deviation $0.05$, and $600$ time steps. The four operators are
selected to approximate the targets $R_A=0.1,1,10,30$, using the corrected
continuous-time variance-kernel criterion derived from the real principal
logarithm of the reduced map. Their computed values are $R_A\simeq
0.099,0.986,9.820,30.424$. The two cases in the left column are below
(or near) the variance-kernel threshold $R_A=1$; the right column is
above it. The same random innovation path is used across the four systems.
(a) Mean field $m_k$, (b) input coordinate $\widehat{\mathbf{n}}^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{x}_k$,
and (c) response coordinate $\widehat{\mathbf{r}}^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{x}_k$.
The response plane is extracted from the known operator by method M2.
For the companion version, the smaller grey annotations give $R_\sigma$,
the exact one-step amplification ratio; the near-threshold $R_A$ case
already has $R_\sigma>1$ because the two thresholds answer distinct questions.}
