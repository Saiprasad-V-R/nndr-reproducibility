\caption{Comparison with established response-plane extraction methods. The reference plane $Q_\star$ is independently planted in each synthetic system. The largest principal angle, $\theta_\star(Q)=\arccos\sigma_{\min}(Q^\top Q_\star)$, is reported in degrees (smaller is better). (a) Dominant planted amplifier. (b) A stronger competing non-normal block. Open and filled circles indicate estimates from $M=1$ and $M=18$ independent training trajectories, respectively. Horizontal whiskers give seed-bootstrap 95\% intervals for the $M=18$ median, based on eight replicated systems per family and method. (c) Planted-plane error for the directly fitted lagged-snapshot method as the horizon $T$ varies, using $M=18$ across three operator families. Curves show medians over eight independently seeded systems. (d) Fraction of the full-system one-step singular gain captured by the inferred plane, $\eta_1(Q)=\sigma_{\max}(Q^\top FQ)/\sigma_{\max}(F)$, for the dominant planted-block family and $M=18$; points denote medians over eight systems. A captured-gain fraction close to one need not imply recovery of a designated planted plane when stronger competing amplifiers are present. The snapshot method directly fits lagged regressions and is not an exact implementation of the specialized methods cited by the referees.}
