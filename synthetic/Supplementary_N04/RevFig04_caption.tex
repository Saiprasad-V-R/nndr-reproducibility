\caption{Coloured forcing and non-normal geometry of the inferred first-order predictor. (a) Exact stationary-population negative controls from the V19 T06 experiment. The physical transition matrix $F$ is normal and has $R_A\simeq0$ under white, isotropically persistent, and spatially structured persistent forcing. The one-step regression operator $F_{\mathrm{reg}}=C_x(1)C_x(0)^{-1}$ remains effectively normal for the first two controls, but its M2 and M3 reductions cross $R_A=1$ for spatially structured persistent forcing. Population M3 reductions are undefined for the normal controls with a degenerate commutator and are therefore omitted rather than plotted as zero. The light diamonds in the companion version show $R_\sigma$ where defined. (b) Raw event exceedance fractions for six separate T06 normal-operator forcing/noise controls (24 independent subjects per family and method, $N=12$, $M=6$ trajectories per subject); horizontal intervals are descriptive 95\% Wilson intervals. The coloured spatial-forcing control yields 21/24 exceedances for both methods, whereas the other five plotted controls have none. Inadmissible rate-based fits remain in the denominators. (c) A separate finite-record experiment reported in the 12 November coloured-forcing note, with normal physical $F$ and $N=12$: $R_A>1$ counts for M2 and M3, each over 216 independently simulated trajectories per forcing persistence $\phi$, pooling forcing orientations, spatial correlation settings, and record lengths. Intervals are descriptive Wilson intervals for the pooled fraction. The companion version adds pale $R_\sigma>1$ fractions; its markers do not alter the $R_A$ denominator. (d) Residual-whiteness rejection fraction in the note's separate $N=2$, $T=500$ experiment, based on 96 valid tests per $\phi$ and 49 first-order bootstrap replicates per test; intervals are descriptive Wilson intervals. The dotted horizontal line denotes 5\%. The T06 and note protocols are not pooled. Apparent amplification of $F_{\mathrm{reg}}$ does not identify non-normality of physical $F$, and residual-model inadequacy does not uniquely identify its cause.}\label{fig:coloured-forcing-controls}
